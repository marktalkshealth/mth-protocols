Part 1

Investigate

About 10 minutes, before any advice is given. We ask about:

  • Symptoms, each scored 0 to 10, so we can measure progress.
  • Tests like A1C, blood pressure, weight and waist, to retest later.
  • Diagnoses, medications and treatments, so your plan works with them.
  • The damage audit: smoking, alcohol, processed food, sleep, screens.
  • The life audit: stress, work, relationships, money and support at home.
  • Your goals, in your own words.

You get your Healing Snapshot: your top problems, your biggest sources of damage, and which condition modules fit you. It's yours to take to your doctor.

Part 3

Learn your condition

A short module for your own condition, unlocked by your Snapshot. Every module covers the same six things:

  • What is happening, in plain words
  • What makes it worse
  • What helps, backed by studies
  • What your doctor can offer, and how lifestyle supports it
  • Red flags that mean see a doctor now
  • What gets added to your daily plan

First modules: Gut Health, Diabetes and GLP-1 weight loss.

Part 4

Heal

The app blends the foundation, your condition protocol and your doctor's treatment into one daily plan, so you never have to work out how the pieces fit. Never more than 3 new actions a week.

Weeks 1–2 · CalmRemove the biggest damage, start sleep and hydration.
Weeks 3–6 · RepairAdd your condition protocol and the first 7-step habits.
Week 7 on · StrengthenBuild all 7 steps into a routine you can keep.
Part 5

Follow up

This is where most programs quietly stop, and where results are made.

  • Weekly check-in (2 minutes): re-score your symptoms and see your trend.
  • Adjust: stuck for two weeks? One small change, not five.
  • Retest at 8 to 12 weeks, and compare side by side.
  • Doctor report: what you changed and how your numbers moved. Any medication change is always your doctor's decision.
