Open my app

First, open this in your browser

You're inside an app's browser, which can't add apps to your home screen.

  1. Tap the ⋯ menu (top or bottom right)
  2. Choose Open in browser
  3. Then follow the steps on this page again

Install on Android

One tap:

A small box pops up. Tap Install, and the app appears on your home screen.

In Chrome:

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app)
  3. Tap Install or Add

Install on iPhone

In Safari (or Chrome), three taps:

  1. Tap the Share button
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add at the top right

In Safari the Share button is at the bottom of the screen; in Chrome it's next to the address bar.

Scan to get it on your phone
